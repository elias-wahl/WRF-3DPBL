## Where things stand

**The 3D boundary-layer closure completes a run for the first time on real terrain.**

Every previous attempt died at model time `2025-07-18_01:38:00` in nocturnal
katabatic drainage on a 33.6° Alpine slope. Job **8476273** ran the full hour,
`01:00` → `02:00`, 1800 steps, exit `0:0`, zero errors, 1.696 s/step.

### What was wrong, physically

The scheme carried **two different eddy sizes at the same grid point**. Turbulent
mixing behaved as though the energy-containing eddies were 2.4 m across; their
decay behaved as though they were 6.0 m.

That cannot describe one eddy. An eddy's size sets both how far it carries
momentum before mixing it in, and how long it survives before passing its energy
down the cascade to smaller scales. Halve the size and you halve the transport
*and* double the decay rate. Using the small size for transport and the large one
for decay produces turbulence that mixes weakly but lives long — which strong
shear never generates.

The strain limiter (which caps eddy size when the mean wind deforms an eddy
faster than it can turn over) correctly found the eddies were smaller. The code
applied that to the stresses and not to the decay.

### What it did to the flow

Turbulence kinetic energy gained roughly 15 % more than it lost, every eddy
turnover, for eight minutes. Compounded, energy doubled every 73 s. Turbulent
gusts reached 6.7 m/s where a nocturnal drainage layer supports 0.1–0.5 m/s. The
downslope wind accelerated seventeenfold instead of being braked by its own
mixing, and the model failed.

### The fix

Commit `db3b9176c`, `dyn_em/module_pbl3d_my.F`, two edits, ~28 lines with
comments. Substance is two lines: when the strain limiter shortens the eddy size,
write that back so the decay rate, the turbulence-energy diffusion coefficients
and the history field all see it. No new parameter, no namelist option, no
Registry change.

A second edit redoes the model-top copy of the length scale, which is made
*before* the per-point solve. Without it the top face keeps the unlimited value
while the level below holds the limited one — silently wrong at one level, no
crash.

## What is open

### 1. The closure carries far too little turbulence energy — and why (the important one)

Measured against the MYNN control (job **8320565**), same grid, forcing, vertical
levels, timestep and surface-layer scheme, both from the same state at 01:00:

| at 02:00 | 3D closure | MYNN control |
|---|---|---|
| `q_sq` (twice turbulence kinetic energy, m² s⁻²), lowest ~100 m | 0.085, still rising linearly | 0.316, equilibrated by 01:30 |
| ratio 3D/MYNN, flat 0–3° / steep 22–40° | 0.14 / 0.36 | — |
| 10 m wind bias 3D − MYNN, flat / steep | −0.32 / +0.56 m s⁻¹ | — |
| median eddy size `l` at 85 m AGL | **0.42 m** | **6.7 m** |
| `l` where `q_sq` sits at its floor (1e-5) | **0.09–0.10 m** | n/a |
| cells at the floor, lowest 5 levels, 01:10 / 01:30 / 02:00 | 65 % / 41 % / 27 % | — |

A 0.42 m eddy at 85 m above ground is not a boundary-layer eddy. It is the value
the length-scale formula returns when it has no turbulence to integrate — and
that is the whole mechanism: in the full 3D path every bound on the eddy size
scales with the turbulence velocity `q = √q_sq`, so small `q` gives a tiny eddy,
which gives tiny stresses (they go as `l q`), tiny production, and `q` stays
small. Two things put the model in that trap and keep it there:

- **The asymptotic length scale collapses to a bare constant.** It is the
  asymptotic-scale constant α = 0.1 times the energy-weighted height centroid of
  the turbulent layer. With `q` at its floor the weighting cancels and it returns
  α itself, 0.1 m, at every level from the surface to the model top. MYNN bounds
  its equivalent to 8–400 m.
- **The run cold-starts at that floor**, `q_sq = 1e-5` everywhere, with the
  friction-velocity seed that would break out of it sitting in dead code. MYNN
  initialises turbulence at local equilibrium instead, and is flat by 01:30.

The length-scale unification is *not* the main cause — it moved `q_sq` by 20–30 %
and the deficit is 3.7×. The fixes (a floor on the asymptotic length scale, an
equilibrium initialisation) and the six experiments that test them are in
`DECISIONS.md`, newest entry.

### 2. The slope factor is applied at the wrong point

`Calc_slope_factor` (`dyn_em/module_pbl3d.F:3033`) builds a factor
`|∇h| · Δx/Δz` — the number of model layers a coordinate surface crosses per grid
cell. Median **5.2** across this domain, up to 21. The lever is the grid, not the
terrain: cells are 500 m wide and 17 m deep, so even a 2° slope gives 1.05 and
10° gives 5.3. It exceeds 2 over **67 %** of the domain and 5 over **51 %**.

**This factor is legitimate and inherited from WRF core** (`smag2d_km` in
`dyn_em/module_diffusion_em.F:2027`, "reduce diffusion coefficient by slope
factor"). It is not an invention of this scheme.

**But WRF applies it to the eddy diffusivity, before the flux is formed.** That is
self-consistent: reduce the diffusivity and the stress falls with it, so the
energy extracted from the mean flow falls by the same factor automatically.

**The 3D scheme applies it to the tendency instead**, after the stress is computed
and after the turbulence has been credited with the full energy transfer. So
subgrid energy appears that the resolved flow never paid for. The factor is fine;
the insertion point is not.

**The fix, when it comes:** mirror WRF's design — apply the factor to the stresses
used for horizontal mixing, before *both* the tendency and the production, rather
than to the tendency alone.

**Do not simply switch it off.** There is a `SLOPE_FACTOR` parameter at
`module_pbl3d.F:3062` that would do so. It was tried this session and reverted:
it is a stability device stock WRF relies on, and the MYNN control run uses the
same factor through `diff_opt=2, km_opt=4`.

Tracked as `branko/OPEN_ISSUES.md` A10.

### 3. The measurement that is actually needed

A subgrid energy-closure diagnostic: compare the kinetic energy the resolved wind
actually loses to the turbulent tendencies against what the turbulence budget
claims it gained. In a consistent closure these are the same quantity, because
the transfer term appears in both equations with opposite signs. Nothing is
produced; energy moves across the filter at one rate.

One Registry field. Requires `--reconfigure` (~30–60 min). **Build it before the
47 h run.** Two attempts this session to size this error from outside the model
both failed — see the corrections below.

**Note what is *not* seen.** The slope-factor defect should leave the closure with
excess turbulence energy on slopes. It has 0.36× the control's there — a deficit,
not an excess. The pairing defect is therefore masked by the energy starvation,
not refuted; it stays open, and the diagnostic (`KE_LOSS_H` against
`QSQ_SHEAR_H`, compared as domain or column integrals) is being built in the next
reconfigure.

## Corrections to the record — things I got wrong

Recorded so they are not rediscovered as facts.

1. **A predicted production/dissipation ratio of ~0.46 measured 24.6.** The
   prediction was linearised — it assumed the flow would reach the same state and
   only the ratio would change. With the fix active from 01:05 the trajectories
   diverge for half an hour first, and that cell never reached the runaway state
   at all. A counterfactual at fixed state is not a forecast.

2. **"59 % of production is spurious" — retracted.** Produced by reconstructing
   the vertical part of production offline from archived stresses and taking the
   residual. Its sanity check gave correlation 0.067 between the reconstruction
   and the model's own production, so the residual was reconstruction error. A
   plausible number from a failed method is worse than no number.

3. **The slope-factor footprint was understated by three orders of magnitude.**
   Described as affecting the 208 points steeper than 30°; it is 67 % of the
   domain.

4. **The slope factor was described as an unphysical patch.** It is a WRF-core
   device. Only its insertion point is wrong here.

5. **A comparison table showed the two runs identical at 01:38.** Stale file — the
   rerun had not yet overwritten the previous run's output. **Filter by
   modification time when comparing against `temp/branko/`.**

6. **The strain limiter's footprint, 4.1 % of cells, was diluted.** It was taken
   over all 80 model levels, most of them free atmosphere. In the lowest five —
   the drainage layer, where the physics is — the limiter binds in **38 %** of
   cells.

7. **RETRACTED 2026-08-21 evening — see the top of this file.** Halving `q_sq`
   left the bias unchanged to 0.01 m s⁻¹, so it is not a turbulence-level effect.
   **The slope-dependent wind bias was called unexplained. It is explained.** It
   needs no slope-dependent cause: it is the turbulence-energy deficit acting on
   two differently forced flows. Turbulence is the *brake* on a locally forced
   drainage wind, so too little of it leaves the 3D run fast on slopes; and the
   *conveyor* that feeds momentum down to a remotely forced valley-floor wind, so
   too little of it leaves the run slow on flat ground. One deficit, two signs.

## Practical traps

- **`build_em_real.sh` always runs `./clean`**, even without `--reconfigure` —
  that is a full 30–60 min rebuild. For a source-only change, build directly:
  `source realcase/env/vsc5.sh && ./compile -j 1 em_real` (~12 min). Keep the job
  count at 1; parallel compilation races. Verify the binary yourself afterwards:
  newer than the edit, and `ldd` clean **with the environment sourced** (Spack
  sets `LIBRARY_PATH` but not `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`, so an unsourced `ldd` shows six
  false "not found" lines).
- **A rerun overwrites `temp/branko/`.** Preserve what you need first.
- **Executable symlinks in the run directory dangle during a build** — that is
  transient, not the stale-symlink trap.
- **`realcase/iofields_qsq.txt` has no blank lines and must keep none** — a blank
  line ends parsing and the diagnostic stream silently empties.
- **The executables exist and are validated** (was: "there are no executables
  right now" — that referred to the interrupted 2026-08-20 18:03 build).
  `main/wrf.exe` and `main/real.exe` were built by the `--reconfigure` that
  finished 2026-08-20 19:41 and relinked at 20:17 after the precision revert;
  `build_em_real.sh` reported **BUILD OK**. With every new switch at its default
  the binary reproduces job 8476273 **bit for bit** — the 01:30 frame of the
  reference run differs by exactly 0 in `U`, `V`, `W`, `T`, `Q_SQ`, `L_MASTER`
  and `PBL3D_T1_RATIO`. Any further Registry change still needs a
  `--reconfigure`; a stale registry gives a binary that runs and writes the new
  fields silently empty.
